How Mudarabah partnership works

Mudarabah pairs capital from the investor with management from the partner. Returns come from real business performance rather than a guaranteed interest rate.

  • No fixed interest (Riba): returns vary with real operating profit
  • Shared risk: investor bears capital loss while manager forfeits time and effort
