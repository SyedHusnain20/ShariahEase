ShariahEase

Islamic Finance, Simplified

ShariahEase is an AI-powered Islamic finance assistant — your personal advisor for Islamic economics, Shariah-compliant finance, and everyday money questions. Ask anything about Riba, Halal investments, or Islamic contracts, then calculate your Zakat and screen halal stocks — all in one place.

Got a question about Mudarabah, an Islamic loan, or whether your savings are Zakat-eligible? Just ask the chatbot in plain Urdu or English and get a clear, scholarly answer. Then calculate your exact Zakat using live gold and silver prices, or check whether a PSX stock is KMI Shariah-compliant — before you invest a single rupee. Free, open, and built for Muslims in Pakistan.
